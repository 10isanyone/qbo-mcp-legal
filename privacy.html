TimeAnalysis — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This policy describes the intended handling of information by TimeAnalysis, a private accounting integration operated by M3 & Associates, LLP ("Firm"). It applies to the Firm's authorized use of the integration, not to the Firm's other services.

1. Information accessed

When an authorized user connects a QuickBooks Online company, TimeAnalysis may retrieve company details, chart-of-accounts information, reports, transactions, and related accounting records needed for an authorized request. These records may include personal or commercially sensitive information contained in the accounting system.

2. How information is used

Information is accessed to produce requested financial reports, answer accounting questions, and support internal review and analysis. The integration is intended to use read-only QuickBooks API operations and is not intended to modify QuickBooks records.

3. Disclosure to service providers

TimeAnalysis uses Intuit's APIs to obtain authorized QuickBooks Online information. When an authorized user requests analysis through ChatGPT, requested records or derived results may be transmitted through the MCP connection to OpenAI for processing in accordance with the applicable service configuration and agreements. Service-provider processing and retention are governed by their applicable terms and settings.

4. Credentials and security

The integration is designed to use Intuit OAuth authorization rather than require users to disclose QuickBooks passwords. Access tokens and other integration credentials must be restricted to authorized administrators and stored outside public code repositories. Access controls, logging, and credential-management procedures will be established as part of deployment. No internet service or data transfer can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Storage and retention

QuickBooks remains the system of record. The integration may process information temporarily or retain limited operational records as required for support and security. ChatGPT interactions and service-provider records may be subject to separate retention settings. The Firm will review retention and deletion controls before enabling production use.

6. Access and revocation

Only authorized personnel should use the integration. Authorized QuickBooks administrators may revoke the application's QuickBooks connection through Intuit's applicable connected-app controls. Users may also contact the Firm about access or data-handling concerns.

7. Changes

This policy may be revised as the integration is deployed or changed. The effective date above identifies this version.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to M3 & Associates, LLP through its website: www.coloradotaxcpa.com.
